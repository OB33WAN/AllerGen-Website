2. Your app records

The rebuilt app stores the information you enter in its private storage on your Android device. This can include:

  • Allergy names, severity levels, custom allergens and household profile names.
  • Food and reaction journal entries, dates, symptoms and notes.
  • Saved ingredient text, scan results, favourite products, meal plans and shopping lists.
  • Medicine names, tablet quantities, reminder intervals and dose-history entries.

The app uses these records to provide the features you choose. Allergy, reaction and medicine information can reveal sensitive information about health. Enter only information you want to store, and only create records for someone else where you have appropriate authority.

The current implementation does not send these health records to an AllerGen server, an AI service or RevenueCat. There is no online health-record account or cloud synchronisation in this release. We cannot retrieve local records for you if your device is lost or the app’s storage is cleared.

3. Camera and label checks

You can take a photo through your camera app, choose an image with Android’s photo picker, or type label text. The photo picker provides access to the image you select; the app does not need access to your entire photo library.

Google ML Kit recognises text on your device. Google states that ML Kit does not send the input images or recognised outputs to its servers. ML Kit may send diagnostic and usage information, such as device and app details, API usage and performance, to Google. See ML Kit’s terms and privacy information.

AllerGen attempts to remove temporary camera captures after processing or cancellation. An interrupted operation can leave temporary data in app storage until it is cleared. Images you chose from your library are not deleted. Ingredient text is retained if you save a scan or product.

4. Medicine reminders

Schedules and reminder delivery are handled locally using Android. Notification permission lets reminders appear; Android also restarts scheduling after a device reboot. Medicine names and dose details may be visible in notifications, including on your lock screen depending on your settings.

You can change notification visibility in Android settings. Deleting all schedules for a medicine removes its associated scheduled records and reminder history in the app. Exported copies are separate. Reminders can be delayed by the operating system and are not a substitute for your medicine instructions.

5. Payments and purchases

Google Play processes payments. AllerGen does not receive your full payment-card details. When billing is configured, RevenueCat checks products, prices, purchase status and entitlement to lifetime access, including when you restore a purchase.

This involves sending purchase history and receipt or purchase-token information, an app-generated customer identifier, and technical information needed to operate the billing service. RevenueCat requests may occur when the app opens, even before you make a purchase. Automatic device-identifier collection is disabled in this app’s RevenueCat configuration; this does not mean no technical information is processed.

Purchase processing supports the service you request and purchase restoration. Records may also be needed to prevent fraud, resolve disputes or meet legal obligations. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Google’s privacy policy. Deleting local app data does not automatically delete records held by those providers.

6. Exports and third parties

When you create or share a PDF or CSV report, it may contain health and medicine information. Reports are generated on your device. Android’s share menu sends the selected report to an app or recipient you choose; AllerGen does not automatically send it to a GP.

Check the contents and recipient before sharing. Copies saved in Downloads, email, cloud drives or another app are controlled separately and are not removed when you delete the original record in AllerGen. Temporary export files may remain in the app cache until cleared.

Opening a link to NHS guidance, another website or Google Play takes you to a third-party service with its own privacy practices. NHS links do not imply endorsement or affiliation.

Google and RevenueCat may process service information outside the UK. Their privacy information explains relevant processing and safeguards; the operator’s applicable transfer arrangements must be verified before launch.

7. This website

This website does not contain advertising trackers, analytics scripts, a newsletter form or a health-data collection form. Its fonts and imagery are served with the website; it does not embed external social widgets or videos.

This site is prepared for hosting on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. Its processing, retention and international-transfer practices are described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. If we change hosting provider, we will update this notice.

If you email us, your email provider and ours process your address, message and any attachments. Please do not include health records, passwords, private keys or card details unless a specific, appropriate process has been agreed.

8. Keeping and deleting your information

Local records stay on your device until you remove them or clear app storage. You can delete journal records and supported items from the relevant app screen. For a complete local reset, open Android Settings → Apps → AllerGen → Storage → Clear storage (wording varies by device). Uninstalling normally removes the app’s private data as well.

This is permanent: clearing storage removes your local profiles, journals and schedules. Save anything you need first. The app disables Android cloud backup and excludes its stored files from its configured device-transfer rules.

To request deletion of information held outside your device, email designs@octopye.com. Explain what you want removed. We may need limited information to identify the relevant purchase or correspondence; never send full card details. Some transaction or legal records may need to be retained. Deletion is not the same as a refund, and may affect purchase restoration.

Before launch, the operator must set and document retention periods for support messages, purchase records and hosting logs. No automatic deletion timetable is promised by this preview notice.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data-protection law gives you rights to ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal information held about you, and to object to certain processing. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it. Some rights have exceptions.

We cannot access health records held only on your device; use the app’s local controls for those records. Contact us for information we or our service providers hold. If you remain concerned, you can raise a concern with the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.

The operator must confirm the lawful bases and, where applicable, special-category conditions for its processing before launch. The app does not make automated medical or treatment decisions.

10. Children, households and carers

A parent, guardian or authorised carer may choose to maintain a local profile for someone they support. Household profiles do not create separate online accounts. Anyone able to open the app on an unlocked device may be able to view its records. Use your device’s lock and appropriate notification-privacy settings.

Do not submit a child’s health information to us by email unless it is necessary and an appropriate process has been agreed.

11. Changes and contact

We will update this notice if the app’s data handling changes. New online, AI, location or synchronisation features require a fresh privacy review before they are introduced.

Questions or data requests: designs@octopye.com.
